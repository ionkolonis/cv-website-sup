"Doombuddy" iOS Study Assistant Application
As part of my IB1 Computer Science Internal Assessment, I was tasked with programming an application to be used to solve a real world problem. After conducting research, I realised that many students of my age struggle to find a balance between their studying and screen time, so I decided to design an application to solve that exact problem. The app is coded in Swift and SwiftUI using Apple's Xcode IDE, and draws inspiration from existing, well-known applications aimed at similar age groups to widen its appeal.
This project is still in progress as of 24/08/2026.
International Law Association Website
As part of my internship at the International Law Association, I was tasked with redesigning the organisation's "outdated" website to give it a more modern UI look. After a month of consistent work, communication with the organisation's staff and several propositions, this design was decided upon.
