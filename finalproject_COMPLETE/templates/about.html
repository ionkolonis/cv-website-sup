About me
My name is Ionas Kolonis (as you can tell from the massive fancy header) and I am a 17 year old student, currently in my last year of school before university.
I was born and spent the first period of my life in Athens, Greece. At the age of two, my family and I moved to London, where I spent what I look back upon as the best years of my childhood. After returning to Athens in 2019, I now hope to return to London for my studies, as I wish to pursue computer science.
As a part of my personality, I like to skim the surface of several different "genres" of activities. At the moment, my main interests include Photography, Fashion, Game Development, Global Politics, Piano, Geography, Football and Karting. When I start something, I tend to get "hooked" very easily, and dedicate hours on end to it without hesitation.
